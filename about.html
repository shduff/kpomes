heya!

while i was away pomes is a collection of poems my partner and i exchanged when apart during the first year of our relationship. he traveled for work, to los angeles and orlando. i traveled for music, playing banjo at an appalachian string music festival in clifftop, west virginia, and tuba at a brass band festival in detroit.

“the language of poetry may well evolve into a 1000 color hieroglyphics”

poetry is important to both of us, and while we were away we wrote to each other almost every day. sometimes about where we were, sometimes about what we were doing, mostly about each other.

“a page is not a four sided white void
in which to practice zeroness.”

the word pome is borrowed from ed sanders, poet, musician, countercultural troublemaker, and founding member of the fugs, who used the spelling as part of a broader rejection of language as something precious or belonging to the academy, insisting instead that it could be irreverent, ordinary, messy, and made by anybody.
