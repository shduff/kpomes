about

while i was away pomes is a collection of poems my partner and i exchanged during the first year of our relationship when apart. he traveled for work, to los angeles and orlando. i traveled for music, playing banjo at an appalachian string music festival in clifftop, west virginia, and tuba at a brass band festival in detroit. poetry is important to both of us, and while we were away we wrote to each other almost every day. sometimes about where we were, sometimes about what we were doing, mostly about each other. the word pome is borrowed from ed sanders, poet, musician, countercultural troublemaker, and founding member of the fugs, who used the spelling as part of a broader rejection of language as something precious or belonging to the academy, insisting instead that it could be irreverent, ordinary, messy, and made by anybody.
